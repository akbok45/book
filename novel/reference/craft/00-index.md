## How to Use These Notes

If writing a novel from scratch, the rough sequence is:

1. Find the premise and the wound at the center of the protagonist (files 01, 03, 10).
2. Pick a structural skeleton (file 02).
3. Choose POV and tense (file 05).
4. Draft scene by scene with stakes (files 04, 08).
5. Layer in setting and dialogue (files 09, 07).
6. Revise (file 11).

Most fiction fails at the level of scene and sentence, not concept. Spend more time there than on outlines.
