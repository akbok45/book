## Chapter 5, The Hidden Village

| # | File | Words | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | `ch05-the-hidden-village/01-up-river.md` | 4918 | Out through the wall, Bonesy walking the jar along the bottom. A Spanish sail on the second day; Jigsaw shows it a course for Jamaica until dark, then doubles back without lights inside the reef, Bonesy calling the coral from the bow. By dawn it is gone, and Crumble agrees to go to Amara. He washes his sash twice. He binds Crumble's torn hands and will not hear that it is nothing. Night watch: counting the finger money, a twist of Jesuits' bark in the surgeon's chest. Crumble takes the watch and sends him to sleep, and he dreams seventeen: the apothecary who is sorry and will not lower the price, the lit houses, the empty sword hooks, the lie to his father, who knows. He wakes with the paper split in his fist and pockets the bark. Over the bar and up a brown river under the trees: no horizon, no wind, howler monkeys, Jigsaw's hand on his hilt, Crumble leading and looking back. The sloop hidden in a creek. On foot to a lake with an island and cooking smoke. A spear at Crumble's neck, and he says Balam's name. |
| 5.2 | `ch05-the-hidden-village/02-the-village.md` | 4786 | Jigsaw draws on the spearmen; Crumble waits. Amara calls them off and knows Jigsaw. Crumble asks to talk to her alone. Nobody fears Bonesy; Jigsaw is the outsider. The walk through the village: the changed people he knew on Tortuga sold fear, these carry water. Ah Pech looks longest at the sign; Amara is called to the sick, and later tells Crumble 'When I'm sure.' Three days: no leaders, no money, Jigsaw's coin on a child's cord; the old man takes him for a Spaniard; the fisherman and his son; the man whose shadow is his way; the man asleep while his way walks; Crumble not wholly at home. A boy falls asleep against Jigsaw during the old man's story, and afterward his hand goes back to his hilt. |
| 5.3 | `ch05-the-hidden-village/03-small-work.md` | 3315 | Crumble names the day in the lake and works Balam's forms; Ah Pech moves his left foot a hand's width without a word. Supper with the coral man's family, Bonesy translating the children; Jigsaw's hand goes to his purse and comes out empty. Next morning he mends their roof and hangs his sword belt on a peg to do it. Bonesy among the old people complaining about joints. Amara at the leaf by lamplight: where does a stone talk? "Go to bed, Spaniard." |
