Draft: $ARGUMENTS

Work in this order. Do not skip step 1 or 2, voice drift comes from writing a
scene in isolation.

1. Read `reference/character-bible.md` for every character in the scene.
2. Read the scene file before it and the scene file after it, whole. If the
   scene is new, read `manuscript/OUTLINE.md` for what it has to carry.
3. Check `notes/open-threads.md` for anything unsettled that this scene would
   decide. If the scene cannot be written without settling one, stop and ask.
   Do not decide plot, kill a character, or invent backstory unasked.
4. Draft into the scene file. A new scene gets a new numbered file in the right
   chapter folder with the same front matter block as its neighbours. If it is
   inserted between two existing scenes, renumber the neighbours.
5. Run `/linecheck` on the new prose before handing it back.
6. Update `words:` in the front matter and the row in `manuscript/OUTLINE.md`.
7. Report in two sentences. Do not recap the content that was just requested.

The prose laws in `CLAUDE.md` override every craft note in `reference/craft/`.
Voice ladder: Bonesy loudest and simplest, Crumble terse and controlled,
Jigsaw dry and a notch under Crumble.
