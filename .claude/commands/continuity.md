Continuity check: $ARGUMENTS

Read `reference/character-bible.md`, then the scene, then the scene on either
side of it. Check against `reference/world-1690.md` for anything the world has
to support.

Check for:
- Facts that contradict the bible: ages, spans of time, names, who knows what
  and when, who was where.
- Facts that contradict an earlier scene.
- Objects, wounds, weapons or gear that appear or vanish without account.
- The same event told two ways inside one scene, which is usually a draft
  artifact from an edit pass rather than a real contradiction.
- Anything already listed in `notes/continuity-flags.md`, so it is not raised
  twice.

`reference/story-so-far.md` is superseded by the bible wherever they disagree.

**Report. Do not fix.** Aydin decides which version is true. For each finding
give the file, the quoted line, what it contradicts and where, and the smallest
change that would settle it. Append anything new to `notes/continuity-flags.md`
under the right heading, hard contradictions separate from draft artifacts and
mechanical slips.

If a finding is a story question rather than an error, it belongs in
`notes/open-threads.md` instead.
